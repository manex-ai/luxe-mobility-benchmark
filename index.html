06 / Useful information

Good to
know.

Are the prices current?

The rates were advertised in September and October 2026. Confirm current rates, availability and fees with Luxe.

How is the estimate calculated?

The estimate multiplies the advertised daily rate by the number of calendar days between pickup and return (minimum one day). Actual billing rules may differ.

What do I need to rent a vehicle?

Confirm licence, age, deposit, insurance and identity requirements directly with the rental company.

Can I take a vehicle outside Harare?

Ask about permitted routes, mileage, insurance and road restrictions before travelling.

Are these actual fleet photos?

No. They are licensed reference photos of related models or generations. Exact vehicle, trim and current condition need verification.
