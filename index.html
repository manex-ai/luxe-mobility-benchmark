Before your trip

Common questions.

Are these prices current?

These are previously advertised rates. Confirm pricing and vehicle availability directly.

What is included in the estimate?

The advertised daily rate multiplied by the calendar days between pickup and return, with a minimum of one day. Actual billing and fees may differ.

What documents do I need?

Confirm licence, age, identification, deposit and insurance requirements with the business.

About the catalogue

Know what you choose.

Are these actual fleet photos?

No. Photos are licensed model-family references. The exact model, trim and condition must be confirmed.

Can I travel outside Harare?

Ask the business about permitted routes, mileage limits and insurance before travel.

Can I book here?

No. This is an independent design demonstration. It prepares an enquiry but does not send messages, process payments or reserve a vehicle.
